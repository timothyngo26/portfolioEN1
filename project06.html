Project 06: Simple Car

Simple Car is a basic design project focused on building a simple vehicle and exploring how straightforward mechanical choices affect motion.

Goals shown in the video:

  • Drive forward (slow and fast)
  • Drive backward (slow and fast)
  • Spin clockwise and counter-clockwise
  • Drive a small arc (circle path with a small radius)
  • Drive a big arc (circle path with a big radius)
