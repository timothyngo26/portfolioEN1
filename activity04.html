Activity 04: Top Spinner

Design a Top Spinner

The motor drives a 36-tooth gear, which transfers motion through a 28-tooth gear and then a 20-tooth gear. This 20-tooth gear is connected by an axle to another 36-tooth gear, which drives a 20-tooth gear connected to the 12-tooth gear on the spinning top. Therefore, the spinner has an overall gear ratio of 3:1.
